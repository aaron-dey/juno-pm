
# Alternative pain point and reasoning 

### Layer 01: User workflow

New deal comittments leads to constant re-prioritisation of roadmap.

Roadmap discussions are driven by the loudest stakeholders from Sales who are either trying to close a deal (RFQ phase), or have closed a deal (contract phase) with the assumption that unsupported use cases or features will be implemented. 

This leads to escalations to leadership, who are under pressure to deliver results that have been promised to the board. 
The impact is that other development that has been planned, needs to be de-prioritised, but because of the high volume of requests and open work in progress it is difficult to fully quantify the impact of changing priorities and defending the reasoning is difficult. 

This leads to further escalations and push to leadership taking prioritisation decisions which are based on gut feel, the loudest stakeholders persuasion or the latests deal details. The impact is tasks remain unfinished, work in progress grows and context switching increases - slows output and velocity. Erodes team motivation.
