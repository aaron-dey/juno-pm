# Strategy: Pricing & Account Tiers

*Rocketship internal strategy doc — effective 2026-09-01*

- Tiers: Tier-1, Tier-2, Tier-3 — defined by contracted seat count and support SLA, never by a PM's estimate of an account's revenue.
- Tier-1 accounts carry a 4-hour first-response SLA on P0 issues. Tier-2 and Tier-3 carry next-business-day.
- Contract renewal freezes: any blocking bug reported by an account within 60 days of contract renewal is elevated to **P1 minimum**, regardless of what its default classification would otherwise be.
- Refunds and service credits require CCO/Finance sign-off. A PM-authored risk report may recommend a credit be considered; it may never commit to one on Rocketship's behalf.
- ARR, deal size, and contract terms are confidential. A risk report cites them only when the source thread explicitly states them — never estimates or infers them.
