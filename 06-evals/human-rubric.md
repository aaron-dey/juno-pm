# Human Evaluation Rubric · Juno P0 Triage Copilot

_Module 6. Generated by `M6 - Human Evaluation Rubric.html`. Paste over `06-evals/human-rubric.md`._

## What graders score

- **Task / product:** Juno P0 Triage Copilot
- **Reviewer audience:** 2 Senior PM + 1 SRE + 1 Support Lead + 1 CSM
- **Value proposition:** Synthesise P0 threads, into top 3 risk categories, so that the on-call PM (or designate) can decide on roll back / defer / ship within 10 minutes.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | 1 (fail) | 3 (ok) | 5 (excellent) |
|---|---|---|---|
| App start | Critical - app does not load at all | Acceptable - app loads within 20s | Excellent - app loads in less than 5s and displays system health  |
| Prioritisation  | Critical - synthesis does not identify any issue | Acceptable - synthesis shows a spread across P0-P3 aligned to the strategy (if given) | Strong - synthesis checks and shows that strategy defined levels are matched and references the specific strategy section and surfaces a user quote |

_Full 1-5 anchors:_

### 1. App start

- **Score 1:** Critical - app does not load at all
- **Score 2:** Sub-par - app takes 30s or more to load
- **Score 3:** Acceptable - app loads within 20s
- **Score 4:** Strong - app loads within 5-10s
- **Score 5:** Excellent - app loads in less than 5s and displays system health 

### 2. Prioritisation 

- **Score 1:** Critical - synthesis does not identify any issue
- **Score 2:** Sub-par - synthesis  assigns P0 to all issues identified
- **Score 3:** Acceptable - synthesis shows a spread across P0-P3 aligned to the strategy (if given)
- **Score 4:** Strong - synthesis checks and shows that strategy defined levels are matched and references the specific strategy section 
- **Score 5:** Strong - synthesis checks and shows that strategy defined levels are matched and references the specific strategy section and surfaces a user quote
