Build insights

What I learned

The honest three: where it hurt, what clicked, and the moment something changed.

🚫 Friction

Iterating and tuning parameters for vector DB ingest, retrieval, and prompt history created the biggest drag.

💡 Learning

Trust comes from system prompts and UX that are consistent, transparent, and controllable from day one.

✨ Aha moment

The trust-gap audit showed that granular human steering and human-in-the-loop prompts are the unlock.
